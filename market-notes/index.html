Market notes

Selected figures from published industry reports on gold, silver and platinum. Reporting periods and publication dates are shown for each metal.

Gold

The World Gold Council reports that central banks bought 289 tonnes of gold in the second quarter of 2026, 62% more than a year earlier. Jewellery consumption fell to 278 tonnes, its lowest quarterly volume since the pandemic, although spending on jewellery rose 14% on the year.

Total demand, second quarter
1,269 tonnes, unchanged on the year
Total demand, first half
2,522 tonnes, up 2%
Central bank purchases
289 tonnes, up 62%
Bar and coin investment
307 tonnes
Exchange-traded funds
Outflows of 45 tonnes
Jewellery consumption
278 tonnes, down 17%
Mine production
966 tonnes, up 2%
Recycling
326 tonnes, down 6%

Source: World Gold Council, Gold Demand Trends: Q2 2026, published 30 July 2026.

Silver

The Silver Institute reports that demand exceeded supply for the fifth consecutive year in 2025 and expects the structural deficit to widen in 2026.

Fifth consecutive annual deficit

1.11 billion oz, down 2%

Source: The Silver Institute, World Silver Survey 2026, released 15 April 2026.

Platinum

WPIC revised its 2026 outlook in its second-quarter report: a surplus is now forecast, following investment outflows and weaker demand. This replaces the deficit forecast in its first-quarter report.

Market balance, 2025 (revised)
Deficit of 1,440,000 oz
Forecast balance, 2026
Surplus of 265,000 oz

7,353,000 oz

Forecast net investment, 2026

About 3.4 months of demand

Source: World Platinum Investment Council, Platinum Quarterly, second quarter 2026, published 9 September 2026.
