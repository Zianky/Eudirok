Services

Physical metals, certified gemstones, independent verification and secure storage, handled under one roof in Dubai.

  • Precious metals trading

    We buy and sell physical gold, silver, platinum and palladium in various forms, including bars, coins and granules. All metals are LBMA-certified and sourced through reputable international refineries and suppliers.

  • Gemstone brokerage

    Our gemstone division trades in certified diamonds and coloured stones, including emeralds, rubies and sapphires, all verified by recognised laboratories such as GIA and IGI.

  • Assay and certification

    We provide independent verification and grading through trusted laboratories to confirm the authenticity and purity of every traded asset.

  • Secure storage and vaulting

    Clients may store their assets in state-of-the-art vaults within Dubai’s free zones, with 24/7 security, full insurance and optional audit reports.

  • Private client services

    For high-net-worth individuals and family offices, we offer discreet portfolio management, including acquisition planning, liquidation strategy and long-term asset preservation.

Trading and advisory

Alongside our core services, we support clients across the full trading cycle.

  • Spot trading and futures contracts
  • Metal investment and portfolio management
  • Physical delivery and warehousing
  • Market research and analysis
  • Customised trading solutions
