Compliance

Trading precious metals and stones responsibly, under UAE law and DMCC regulation.

Trust through transparency

Eudirok Jewellery operates under UAE Federal Law and Dubai Multi Commodities Centre (DMCC) regulations.

We strictly adhere to Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) guidelines. All trades are subject to international best practice for traceability, ethical sourcing and environmental responsibility.

Our compliance policy ensures

  • Full client identification and verification
  • Certified sourcing from conflict-free regions
  • Complete documentation for every transaction
  • Secure and insured logistics
