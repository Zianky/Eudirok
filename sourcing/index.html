Responsible gold sourcing

Every purchase of physical gold follows twelve documented steps.

We buy only from counterparties who can show that they own the gold, may lawfully sell it and may lawfully export it. No step begins until the one before it is complete.

The twelve steps

  1. 01

    Initial commercial contact

  2. 02

    Preliminary compliance review

  3. 03

    Exchange of corporate documentation

  4. 04

    Commercial negotiation

  5. 05

    Sales and purchase agreement

  6. 06

    Payment security

  7. 07

    Documentary verification

  8. 08

    Shipment authorisation

  9. 09

    Delivery to refinery

  10. 10

    Independent assay

  11. 11

    Commercial settlement

  12. 12

    Transaction closure

What suppliers must demonstrate

  • They are legally established and have the capacity to sell
  • The gold was lawfully acquired and they are authorised to sell it
  • The export is authorised by the competent authorities
  • Royalties, taxes and export duties have been paid where required
  • Every document submitted is authentic, complete and accurate

What we will not trade

  • Stolen or illegally mined gold
  • Smuggled goods and unauthorised exports
  • Counterfeit documents or fraudulent ownership claims
  • Anything subject to applicable sanctions
  • Transactions meant to conceal the origin of assets or funds

Intermediaries are welcome where their role and authority are disclosed and documented. Undisclosed intermediaries are not accepted.
