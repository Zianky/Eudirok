Responsible gold sourcing

Gold enquiries are assessed case by case, with attention to counterparties, origin and transaction documents.

Before a transaction proceeds, the parties agree its commercial terms and identify the information and documents needed for that specific product, route and risk profile. Review may include legal identity and beneficial ownership, authority to sell or export, available origin and product records, and relevant permits or declarations.

Where applicable, delivery, independent assay, acceptance criteria and settlement conditions are documented in the transaction agreement. Requirements vary by transaction and jurisdiction.

Transaction review may cover

  • Counterparty identity, ownership and authority
  • Product description and available origin information
  • Export and import documents applicable to the route
  • Sanctions and other risk-based checks
  • Delivery, assay and settlement terms

Document integrity

Documents are subject to review and independent verification where appropriate. Website statements or copies of documents are not a substitute for transaction-specific checks with issuing authorities, banks, carriers, laboratories and other relevant parties.

No transaction is represented as approved until its required checks and contractual conditions are completed.
