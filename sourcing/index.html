Responsible gold sourcing

Gold enquiries are assessed case by case, with attention to counterparties, origin and transaction documents.

Before a transaction proceeds, the parties agree its commercial terms and identify the information and documents needed for that specific product, route and risk profile. Review may include legal identity and beneficial ownership, authority to sell or export, available origin and product records, and relevant permits or declarations.

Where applicable, delivery, independent assay, acceptance criteria and settlement conditions are documented in the transaction agreement. Requirements vary by transaction and jurisdiction.

Transaction review may cover

  • Counterparty identity, ownership and authority
  • Product description and available origin information
  • Export and import documents applicable to the route
  • Sanctions and other risk-based checks
  • Delivery, assay and settlement terms

Document integrity

Documents are subject to review and independent verification where appropriate. Website statements or copies of documents are not a substitute for transaction-specific checks with issuing authorities, banks, carriers, laboratories and other relevant parties.

No transaction is represented as approved until its required checks and contractual conditions are completed.

The UAE framework for responsible gold sourcing

The Ministry of Economy’s due diligence regulations for responsible sourcing of gold are based on OECD guidance. They have applied to gold refineries operating in the UAE since January 2023 and follow five steps.

  1. 1

    Management systems

    Set up management systems, including a compliance officer who reports directly to the chief executive, staff training and checks on suppliers before a relationship starts.

  2. 2

    Identify risks

    Assess supply chain risks, identify risk indicators and report continuously to senior management.

  3. 3

    Respond to risks

    Design a strategy to manage the risks found and a plan to monitor them.

  4. 4

    Independent review

    Commission third-party reviews of due diligence practices under an audit plan.

  5. 5

    Annual reporting

    Submit supply chain due diligence audit reports to the Ministry every year, within 90 days of completing the review cycle.

Red flags in a gold enquiry

  • Gold in unrefined or doré form without verifiable origin documents
  • Suppliers or routes linked to jurisdictions on the FATF high-risk lists
  • Sourcing from conflict-affected areas

These regulations place obligations on gold refiners and other controlled businesses. This summary is general information, not a statement of how they apply to any one company or transaction. See also the UAE gold market. Source: Khaleej Times, 13 September 2022, reporting the Ministry’s publication of the regulations.
