About us

Eudirok Jewellery is a precious metals trading company based in Dubai. We buy, sell and store gold, silver, platinum and palladium, and give institutional and private clients transparent, secure and efficient access to the physical precious metals and stones markets.

What sets us apart is analytical expertise, strict compliance standards and personal service. Through our global network and careful risk management, we help partners diversify their portfolios, hedge against inflation and act on market movements.

Mission
To give clients the tools, insights and security they need to make informed decisions in the precious metals market.
Vision
To be the global leader in precious metal trading, setting standards for quality and service.

Services

From buying and selling to assay, vaulting and private client work.

  • Precious metals trading

    Physical gold, silver, platinum and palladium as bars, coins and granules, including raw gold and other precious metals. Metals are LBMA-certified and sourced through international refineries and suppliers.

  • Diamonds, pearls and precious stones

    Trading in certified diamonds, pearls and coloured stones, including emeralds, rubies and sapphires, verified by recognised laboratories such as GIA and IGI.

  • Jewellery trading

    New and used jewellery and accessories made of gold and silver.

  • Import and export

    Cross-border trade in precious metals, stones and jewellery, with documented and insured logistics.

  • Assay and certification

    Independent verification and grading through trusted laboratories, to confirm the authenticity and purity of what we trade.

  • Private client services

    Discreet purchase and sale of physical metals and stones for high-net-worth individuals and family offices, including acquisition planning and liquidation.

  • Research and tailored solutions

    Market research and analysis, and trading solutions shaped around each client.
