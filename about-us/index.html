About us

A Dubai-based precious metals trading business serving professional and corporate counterparties.

A trading house for clients who want the physical asset

Eudirok Jewellery's company profile describes a Dubai-based precious metals trading business focused on gold, silver and platinum group metals. It identifies professional and corporate counterparties, including refineries, authorised dealers and institutional traders, and states that the business is not retail-facing. Product specifications, documentation, logistics, assay and settlement terms are agreed for each transaction.

For gold transactions, we review counterparty identity, ownership and authority to sell or export, alongside transaction documents and product details. The checks depend on the transaction and applicable requirements.

We aim to agree the scope, documents and transaction terms before proceeding. Market information is provided for context and is not a guarantee of price or investment performance.
