Cookie policy

This website sets no cookies. Here is what that covers and what loads from other companies. Last updated 23 October 2026.

Our use of cookies

A cookie is a small file that a website stores on your device. Websites use cookies, and similar tools such as local storage, to remember you, measure visits or show advertising.

This website does none of that. It sets no cookies and writes nothing to your browser’s storage, so there is no cookie banner to accept or decline.

What loads from other companies

The services below may receive a request depending on the page you open and the actions you take:

  • Google Fonts delivers the site’s two typefaces. Google receives your IP address and browser details in order to send the font files, and its own privacy policy applies.
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) delivers selected gold and silver images on the home page and photographs in the buyer’s guide. It receives technical request data such as your IP address and browser details. Your browser is told not to send the referring page. The photographers are listed in image credits.
  • Pexels delivers the gold-bar video only if you choose to play it. Pexels receives technical request data when the video is requested; the source and creator are listed on the image credits page.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when a page is requested.

The decorative background videos and most pictures are hosted with this website. The exceptions are the Unsplash images and the Pexels video described above. The map link on the contact page opens Google Maps only if you select it. The external video file is not requested until you press play. Google’s terms apply when Maps opens.

We do not control what these companies record or store on their own systems.

Your choices

You do not need to accept or decline anything on this website. Your browser lets you block cookies and requests to other companies. If you block Google Fonts, the website still works and shows your device’s standard typefaces. If you block Unsplash or Pexels, the website still works; the affected image or video may not appear.

If we ever add a tool that uses cookies or similar technology, such as visitor statistics, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

For questions, write to info@eudirok.com. The privacy notice explains how we handle information you send us.
