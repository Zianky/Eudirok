Cookie policy

This website sets no cookies. Here is what that covers and what loads from other companies. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Our use of cookies

A cookie is a small file that a website stores on your device. Websites use cookies, and similar tools such as local storage, to remember you, measure visits or show advertising.

This website does none of that. It sets no cookies and writes nothing to your browser’s storage, so there is no cookie banner to accept or decline.

What loads from other companies

Two outside companies receive a request when you open a page:

  • Google Fonts delivers the site’s two typefaces. Google receives your IP address and browser details in order to send the font files, and its own privacy policy applies.
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) delivers some photographs on the home page and in the page headers of the inner pages. Unsplash receives your IP address and browser details in order to send the pictures. Your browser is told not to say which page you came from. Unsplash’s own privacy policy applies, and the photographers are named on the image credits page.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when a page is requested.

The videos, scripts and most pictures come from this website itself. The only exception is the Unsplash photographs described above. The map link on the contact page opens Google Maps only if you select it, and Google’s terms apply on that site.

We do not control what these companies record or store on their own systems.

Your choices

You do not need to accept or decline anything on this website. Your browser lets you block cookies and requests to other companies. If you block Google Fonts, the website still works and shows your device’s standard typefaces. If you block Unsplash, the website still works and shows plain dark panels where its photographs would be.

If we ever add a tool that uses cookies or similar technology, such as visitor statistics, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

For questions, write to info@eudirok.com. The privacy notice explains how we handle information you send us.
