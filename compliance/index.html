Governance and compliance

A transaction-specific approach to precious-metals due diligence.

How we approach transactions

Each proposed transaction is subject to counterparty and ownership checks, review of product and origin information, and verification of relevant trade and export documents. The checks required depend on the parties, product, route, risk and applicable law.

Commercial terms, delivery, any independent assay and settlement arrangements must be agreed in the transaction documents. A website description does not certify a transaction, product, licence or compliance status.

The company profile describes internal KYC/AML/CTF, sanctions and transaction-monitoring procedures. The existence of internal procedures does not by itself demonstrate their implementation or establish regulatory compliance.

Selected official UAE references

  • Federal Decree by Law No. 10 of 2025: AML/CFT and proliferation-financing framework
  • Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025: executive regulations under the AML/CFT law
  • Federal Law No. 11 of 2015: trade in precious stones and metals and stamping
  • Cabinet Resolution No. 45 of 2018: executive regulations on precious-stone and metal trade and stamping
  • Cabinet Resolution on reverse-charge VAT for qualifying precious metals and stones transactions
  • Ministry of Economy and Tourism: responsible sourcing of gold
  • Ministry of Economy and Tourism: AML/CFT resources and guidance

These are selected references, not a complete list of legal obligations. Applicability depends on the licensed activities, product, transaction, origin and destination. This page is general information, not a legal opinion or a representation that Eudirok has been independently verified as compliant.

Before a transaction

Counterparties may be asked for corporate and beneficial-ownership information, authorised signatory details, product and origin records, and permits or declarations applicable to the route and transaction.

Current licence scope, regulatory registrations, responsible-sourcing obligations, customs and tax requirements, sanctions screening and product-specific rules should be confirmed against official records and the specific transaction before commitments are made.
