Governance and compliance

Licensed in Dubai and run under a written purchasing policy.

Trust through transparency

We are licensed by the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism and operate under UAE federal law. Our purchasing activities are governed by a written Corporate Gold Purchasing Policy, version 1.0, effective July 2026, issued by the Board of Directors and approved by the Managing Director.

Every trade follows international best practice for traceability, ethical sourcing and environmental responsibility.

We maintain a strict zero-tolerance approach toward illegal mining, conflict minerals, corruption, money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions violations and fraudulent commercial practices.

How the company is run

Operations, compliance oversight and financial control are kept separate. No single department can authorise a transaction outside its delegated authority.

Sets strategic direction, approves corporate policies and oversees long-term objectives.

Know your counterparty

Before any commitment we identify the legal entity we are dealing with. The review is risk-based: it scales with transaction value, country of origin, ownership structure, the involvement of intermediaries and the quality of the documents.

The standard package covers corporate registration and trade licence, constitutional documents, an ultimate beneficial owner declaration, identification of authorised signatories and bank details. Higher-risk cases go through enhanced due diligence, and licences, ownership and signatories are rechecked for as long as the relationship lasts.

Controlled settlement

Payment is released only after the compliance review is complete, the contract documents are verified, delivery conditions are confirmed and, where applicable, the refinery has verified the material. We do not recognise informal payment arrangements or undisclosed payment instructions.

The full policy

The policy manual also covers risk management, the code of conduct, anti-bribery and anti-corruption, information security and data protection, whistleblowing, business continuity and internal audit. It is a confidential document. Qualified suppliers, refineries, banks and authorities can request a copy.
