Services

Precious metals trading for professional counterparties.

  1. Precious metals trading

    Gold, silver and platinum group metals for professional and corporate counterparties. Product form, specifications and available supporting documents are confirmed for each enquiry.

  2. Cross-border transactions

    Import and export arrangements depend on product, route, applicable requirements and transaction documents. The parties, responsibilities and logistics are agreed in writing.

  3. Assay and settlement coordination

    Independent assay and settlement steps may be agreed for a transaction. The assay provider, scope, delivery point, acceptance criteria and payment terms should be specified in the relevant agreement.

  4. Transaction documentation

    Commercial terms and payment arrangements are documented for each transaction and remain subject to due diligence and applicable requirements.

Transaction-specific controls

Counterparty identity, authority, product details, origin and export documents are reviewed as relevant to a transaction. Delivery, assay and payment conditions are agreed in the transaction documents.

  • Counterparty and authority checks
  • Origin, export and product documentation
  • Agreed delivery, assay and settlement conditions

Kilograms, ounces and tolas

Convert common precious-metals weight units. Transaction units and tolerances are specified in the relevant offer or agreement.

1 troy ounce = 31.1035 g. 1 tola = 11.6638 g.
