Privacy notice

How we handle information about you, whether you only visit this website, write to us or trade with us. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Eudirok Jewellery L.L.C. S.O.C. (“Eudirok”, “we”), trade licence 1553655, Abstract Business Center L.L.C., Building 06, Oud Metha, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We decide how the information described here is used.

For any question about this notice, or to use any of your rights, write to info@eudirok.com.

What we collect

When you visit the website. We run no analytics and set no cookies. When a page loads, the companies that host the site, its typefaces and some of its photographs (GitHub, Google and Unsplash) receive technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. We do not collect this ourselves. The cookie policy names these companies.

When you write to us. Your name, email address, message and anything else you include, or your phone number and what you tell us if you call. The enquiry form on the contact page does not send anything from this website. It prepares an email on your own device, and nothing reaches us until you send that email from your email app.

When you trade with us. The details we need to know who we are dealing with: corporate registration and trade licence, constitutional documents, an ultimate beneficial owner declaration, identification of authorised signatories, bank details, and the records of each transaction. The compliance page describes these checks.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • To carry out and document a trade, including payment, assay, delivery and storage
  • To identify and verify counterparties and screen them against sanctions lists, as UAE anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing and sanctions rules require
  • To keep records, and to answer the authorities when the law requires it
  • To protect the business against fraud

Where the law requires your consent for a use, we ask for it, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Who receives it

Only the people who need it for the purposes above: banks and payment providers, refineries, assay laboratories, logistics and insurance providers, professional advisers, and UAE authorities where the law requires, for example in reports to the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit.

Our clients, banks and refineries are in many countries, so some information may be sent outside the UAE when a trade needs it. We limit this to what the trade or the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above. Records of trades and of the checks we made on counterparties are kept for as long as UAE anti-money-laundering law requires, which can be several years after a relationship ends. Enquiries that do not lead to a trade are deleted when we no longer need them.

Your rights

Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, and under similar laws in other countries, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you and why
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete information we no longer need
  • Restrict or stop a use you object to
  • Give you a copy of it so you can move it elsewhere
  • Stop relying on your consent, where we asked for it

The website makes no automated decisions about you.

To use a right, write to info@eudirok.com. We will reply, and if the law or our compliance obligations mean we must keep a record, we will tell you which one and why. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the UAE Data Office or to the data protection authority where you live.

Security, children and changes

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the information we hold, and we limit access to the people who need it.

This website is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

When this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
