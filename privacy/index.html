Privacy notice

What this website does with your information. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Eudirok Jewellery L.L.C. S.O.C., Abstract Business Center L.L.C., Building 06, Oud Metha, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For anything in this notice, write to info@eudirok.com.

What the website collects

The website has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It does not set cookies of its own, and it does not store what you type into it.

The enquiry form on the contact page does not send anything from this website. It prepares an email on your own device. When you choose to send it, the email travels through your own email provider to info@eudirok.com, and your name, email address and message reach us in that way.

How we use an enquiry

We use the details in an enquiry to reply to you. If we go on to trade together, we also use them for the identification and verification steps described on our compliance page.

Third parties

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives the technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, as any web host does. The site’s typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when a page loads. The photographs, videos and scripts are served from this site and not from other companies.

The map link on the contact page opens Google Maps only when you select it.

Your information

To ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to ask us to delete it, write to info@eudirok.com. Where the law or our compliance obligations require us to keep a record, we will tell you.

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
